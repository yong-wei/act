## Why

The dated audit records exact-name conflicts and unresolved near-similar evidence. Before semantic blocks can be assigned, one global manifest must distinguish hard identity equivalence from review-only similarity.

## What Changes

- Generate globally closed identity equivalence components only from exact normalized names and reviewed controlled aliases.
- Emit near-similar review edges only between distinct components; dated snapshot counts remain proposal evidence.
- Define pending split as an internal component disposition without pre-splitting ownership or historical evidence.
- Prohibit automatic canonical merge, final semantic naming, or final split approval.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `knowledge-identity-candidate-manifest`: defines exhaustive identity equivalence components, review-only near-similar edges, pending internal split dispositions, and history-mapping ambiguity records.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Produces an offline candidate manifest consumed by semantic partitioning.
- Does not modify canonical identities, runtime nodes, resources, learning evidence, or production projections.
## ADR 0045 Boundary

Historical mapping input is limited to reviewed active legacy knowledge IDs needed by current course/resource/progress/note or incomplete-path references. Historical facts and completed paths are not remapped, replayed, deduplicated, or backfilled.
