## Why

The current SAR diagnostics include a deterministic control-correction demo fixture. That proves the multi-hop teaching case is reproducible, but competition and product evaluation need live or structured evidence: query history, comparison against ordinary retrieval, verified citation rate, privacy rejection counts, path-planner SAR adoption, and at least two user or structured test feedback records.

## What Changes

- Add a SAR live evaluation report derived from persisted traces and governed diagnostics.
- Compare SAR-assisted retrieval with ordinary Source Pack or hybrid retrieval on representative teaching queries.
- Report multi-hop hit rate, verified citation rate, privacy rejections, Source Pack handoff, candidate adoption/rejection, and known limitations.
- Include at least two target-user feedback records or structured test records.

## Impact

- Extends `structured-associative-retrieval`.
- Extends `admin-data-governance-dashboard`.
- Depends on persisted traces and refresh health.
- Does not create new retrieval algorithms or bypass citation verification.
