## Why

Reports, governance snapshots, and export surfaces must carry the same premium identity and evidence semantics as the app, but they also need print/export readability, watermark discipline, privacy scope, and source labels. These requirements are different enough from operations consoles to require a separate change.

## What Changes

- Define report-ledger archetype for classroom, Arena, learner, governance, and data-center report surfaces.
- Apply brand watermark, source quality, freshness, privacy, evidence/status legend, and export readability rules.
- Add validation for screenshot, print, or export-oriented evidence without obscuring charts, tables, formulas, or student identifiers.
- Register the concrete report-ledger route or component inventory before changing report/export presentation.
- Keep report-ledger ownership limited to report/export/snapshot presentation unless route ownership is explicitly transferred.
- Require classroom, Arena, learner, governance, and data-center examples to preserve source, timestamp, privacy, confidence, and official/preview labels.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `platform-commercial-brand-language`: adds report watermark and report brand application requirements.
- `platform-data-center-ui`: adds snapshot/export presentation requirements.
- `platform-status-and-evidence-ui`: adds report/export status and privacy requirements.

## Impact

- Affects report-like UI, governance/data-center snapshots, exported/print views, watermark assets, evidence/status legends, and visual QA artifacts.
