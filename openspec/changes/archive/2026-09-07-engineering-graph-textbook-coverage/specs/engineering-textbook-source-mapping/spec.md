## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mappings target textbooks-v2 structural-unit coordinates
Every canonical→textbook mapping MUST resolve to a textbooks-v2 structural-unit coordinate: `bookId`, `edition`, `structuralUnitId`, and `structuralPath` of a unit present in the v2 runtime manifest. The coordinate MUST be the same identity used by the reader href and the hybrid retrieval index. Legacy v1 SourceAnchor locator rows MAY remain consumable, but new mapping rows MUST carry v2 coordinates.

#### Scenario: Mapping row carries v2 coordinates
- **WHEN** a mapping row is admitted to the governed ledger
- **THEN** it SHALL record the target unit's `bookId`, `edition`, `structuralUnitId`, and `structuralPath`
- **AND** the coordinate SHALL resolve to an existing unit in the target book's v2 runtime manifest

#### Scenario: Coordinate does not resolve
- **WHEN** a mapping row names a `structuralUnitId` or `structuralPath` absent from the v2 runtime manifest
- **THEN** the row SHALL fail closed
- **AND** it SHALL NOT enter the governed ledger or runtime consumption

### Requirement: Candidates come from retrieval recall, not fabrication
Mapping candidates MUST be generated by recalling structural units from the textbook hybrid retrieval index using each canonical object's labels, aliases, and teaching fields, augmented by the governed retrieval-term dictionary. String-similarity or vector-similarity alone MUST NOT produce an approved mapping.

#### Scenario: Candidate set is produced for an object
- **WHEN** the pipeline processes a canonical object
- **THEN** it SHALL recall candidate structural units from the hybrid retrieval index
- **AND** each candidate SHALL record its recall query and rank for audit

#### Scenario: Similarity without review
- **WHEN** a candidate has high retrieval rank but no independent semantic review verdict
- **THEN** it SHALL NOT become an approved mapping

### Requirement: Independent semantic review ledger governs approval
Every candidate mapping MUST receive an independent semantic review verdict (`approved` or `rejected` with a reason) recorded in an append-only review ledger before it may be consumed. Ledger entries MUST be immutable once written; corrections SHALL be new entries.

#### Scenario: Candidate is approved
- **WHEN** a reviewer verdict approves a candidate
- **THEN** the ledger SHALL append the verdict with reviewer identity, reason, and the candidate coordinate
- **AND** the mapping SHALL become eligible for runtime consumption

#### Scenario: Ledger entry is edited in place
- **WHEN** an attempt is made to mutate an existing review verdict
- **THEN** the pipeline SHALL fail closed
- **AND** the correction SHALL only be expressible as a new appended entry

### Requirement: Unmapped nodes enter an explicit exception ledger
A canonical object without any approved mapping MUST enter an explicit exception ledger with a classified reason. Silent absence of a mapping MUST NOT occur; approved mappings plus ledgered exceptions MUST account for 100% of the mapping denominator.

#### Scenario: Object has no approved mapping
- **WHEN** all candidates for an object are rejected or no candidate exists
- **THEN** the object SHALL enter the exception ledger with a classified reason
- **AND** the exception SHALL count toward the coverage denominator

#### Scenario: Object is absent from both ledgers
- **WHEN** the coverage gate evaluates an object present in neither the approved mappings nor the exception ledger
- **THEN** the gate SHALL fail closed

### Requirement: Coverage gate is fail-closed at the 95% line
The combined coverage of approved mappings and explicit exceptions MUST reach at least 95% of the 7476-object engineering graph denominator before runtime consumption may switch to the new crosswalk. Coverage SHALL be reported per component domain; falling below the gate MUST fail closed.

#### Scenario: Coverage meets the gate
- **WHEN** approved mappings plus explicit exceptions cover at least 95% of the denominator
- **THEN** the governed crosswalk SHALL become eligible for runtime consumption

#### Scenario: Coverage falls below the gate
- **WHEN** combined coverage is below 95%
- **THEN** the pipeline SHALL fail closed
- **AND** runtime consumers SHALL retain the prior textbook channel until human review authorizes an exemption

### Requirement: Mapping authority binding is pinned to the active v0.37 release
New crosswalk rows MUST bind the active Authority release `ctr:release:control-theory-engineering-v0.37` identity (`authorityReleaseId`, `authorityReleaseHash`, `bundleDigest`, `captureRevision`). Rows pinned to a superseded release such as v0.12 MUST NOT enter runtime consumption.

#### Scenario: Row binds the active release
- **WHEN** a new crosswalk row is admitted
- **THEN** its authority binding fields SHALL equal the active v0.37 release identity
- **AND** the binding SHALL be validated against the current authority pointer

#### Scenario: Row binds a superseded release
- **WHEN** a crosswalk row carries a v0.12 or otherwise stale authority binding
- **THEN** the row SHALL fail closed
- **AND** it SHALL NOT be consumed by the runtime textbook channel
