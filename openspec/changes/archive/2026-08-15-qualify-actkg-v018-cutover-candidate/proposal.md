## Why

Candidate files and a rebuilt Teaching Projection do not prove that all ACT
consumers can switch coherently. A dedicated qualification stage must join the
frozen Authority, localized display projection, Teaching Projection,
prerequisites, and six consumer manifests without changing production pointers.

## What Changes

- Assemble one immutable cutover candidate that pins the v0.18 Authority,
  localized-label index, Teaching Projection, prerequisite publication,
  composed Authority domain-shard set, and all six named consumer records.
- Run the complete v0.9 to v0.18 object/relation/reference audit, Chinese label
  queries, teaching queries, graph/RAG/Konling/course/card/path shadow reads,
  and two independent snapshot rebuilds.
- Require every captured teaching reference to resolve or carry an explicit
  reviewed mapping; terminology coverage remains informational and additive.
- Exercise an identity-constrained v0.18 to v0.9 rollback in an isolated
  non-default five-selector control root and prove all real current pointers
  remain unchanged.
- Emit a signed readiness report whose only successful next action is runtime
  publication; it cannot activate production.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `actkg-v018-cutover-qualification`: produce immutable, non-activating evidence
  that the complete v0.18 Authority and ACT teaching release set is deployable and reversible.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Adds candidate manifests, shadow-query fixtures, deterministic rebuild proof,
  rollback rehearsal, readiness reports, and qualification gates.
- Depends on `project-actkg-v018-zh-cn-labels` and
  `rebase-act-teaching-projection-to-v018`.
- Does not build a production image, deploy a server, or change production selectors.
