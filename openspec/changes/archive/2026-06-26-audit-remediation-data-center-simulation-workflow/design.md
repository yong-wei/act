## Context

Data Center and simulation findings are operational workflow gaps. They need role/source boundaries, export reachability, visible simulation tools, mission context preservation, and save-back status. They do not require resource graph or numerical panel rewrites.

## Design

1. Data Center boundaries.
   - Student access routes to evidence/review contexts with an explicit reason.
   - Teacher/admin Data Center surfaces distinguish demo, synthetic, imported, and production-backed data through visible or inspectable source quality states.

2. Export and governance handoff.
   - Export buttons are not obstructed by global floating controls.
   - Export states include preparing, ready, downloaded, failed, and retry.
   - Snapshot exports declare source table families, source window, requester role, source quality, and redaction policy.
   - Raw learning facts, competency snapshots, risk flags, class snapshots, direct student ids, and private evidence bodies are excluded unless explicitly authorized for the requester role and recorded in the artifact scope.
   - Teacher/admin governance handoffs keep origin route and target scope.

3. Simulation workspace.
   - Shell-declared bottom tools must be visible or explicitly unsupported with a recovery reason.
   - Mission-linked simulation routes preserve mission title, objective, completion criteria, and return/save-back target.
   - Simulation directory filters announce result changes, empty states offer recovery, and `/virtual-lab` compatibility redirects explain the destination.

4. Portfolio and task return.
   - Simulation design and task flows state whether work is saved, queued for review, or unsupported.

## Out Of Scope

- Graph resource grounding, KAQ writeback, adaptive path planning, and Rust numerical-panel implementation.
