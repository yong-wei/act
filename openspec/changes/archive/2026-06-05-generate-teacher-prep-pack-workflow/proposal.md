## Why

The teaching-assistant report's strongest product claim is that analysis changes the next class. Existing and future teacher reports can show what happened, but teachers also need an actionable next-class enhancement package that fits the course plan instead of producing disconnected AI suggestions.

## What Changes

- Add a teacher prep-pack generation contract from class diagnosis, path outcomes, grading summaries, ResourceNodes, and next lesson plan context.
- Generate candidate interactive questions, knowledge cards, micro-simulations, Arena tasks, reflection prompts, and Konling prompts with evidence and confidence metadata.
- Require teacher review before a prep pack is inserted into class sessions or exported.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `teacher-prep-pack-generation`

## Impact

- Turns diagnosis into teacher action.
- Consumes future control-correction teacher reports, role-based diagnosis, path policy families, and grading summaries when available.
- Does not automatically rewrite course content or lesson plans without teacher approval.
