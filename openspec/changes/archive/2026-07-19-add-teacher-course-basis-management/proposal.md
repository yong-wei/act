## Why

Smart preparation cannot claim to follow teacher-selected standards and textbooks until teachers can introduce, review, version, and privately retrieve their own materials. ACT has governed runtime retrieval but no general teacher upload and extraction workflow.

## What Changes

- Add teacher-private reusable course bases and immutable document versions.
- Accept searchable PDF, Markdown, plain text, and pasted text with extraction preview and stable anchors.
- Reject scan-only PDFs and unsupported formats without OCR.
- Project only teacher-confirmed versions into governed `lesson-design` Source Pack retrieval.
- Provide the ordinary-import Hu Shousong root-locus material and compact course-standard demo basis with machine-readable rights/provenance, without publicly redistributing restricted textbook text.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `teacher-course-basis-management`: private source ingestion, extraction review, stable anchors, immutable versions, authorization, and governed retrieval projection.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Adds Prisma records, teacher workspace/API surfaces, extraction storage, a searchable-PDF adapter, governed corpus projection, rights/provenance evidence, and authorization tests.
- Does not add lesson generation, courseware generation, publication, OCR, or cross-teacher sharing.
