## Why

Teacher, admin, grading, preparation, runtime overlay, governance, and report surfaces are operational products. They need dense repeated-use navigation, role context, evidence/status semantics, and export/report handling. The future runtime enhancement pack overlay and assistant demo/effect report changes will increase the need for one operations/report shell language.

## What Changes

- Migrate teacher and admin operations routes to the `operations-console` archetype.
- Migrate grading, preparation pack, governance snapshots, and effect report representative surfaces to `report-ledger` where evidence review or export is primary.
- Preserve overlay preview/review/activate/archive/rollback semantics and assistant demo/effect report deterministic evidence.
- Provide honest unavailable states for future analytics and model/system governance slots.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `teacher-admin-governance-workspaces-ui`
- `commercial-workspace-surface-system`

## Impact

- Depends on `upgrade-platform-app-shell-to-archetype-shell`.
- Soft-aligns with `runtime-enhancement-pack-overlay` and `assistant-closeloop-demo-effect-report`.
- Does not migrate mission workspaces or learner/knowledge/data surfaces.
