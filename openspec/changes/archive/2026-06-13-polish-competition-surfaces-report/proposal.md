## Why

Even if the assistant loop works technically, the competition submission will fail to communicate value if the core pages remain visually fragmented or the effect report cannot be exported from source-backed metrics. This change is the final competition polish layer after the baseline, evidence loop, grading, path, and prep-pack work are in place.

## What Changes

- Require all competition core routes to conform to unified AppShell or approved workspace shell governance.
- Capture 1440px desktop and 320px mobile evidence for teacher, student, administrator, grading, diagnosis, path, prep-pack, and effect-report surfaces.
- Produce a source-backed assistant effect report with grading, diagnosis, path, prep-pack, citation, and usage metrics.
- Generate competition asset manifest, demo script, screenshot ledger, privacy note, model note, and release-candidate checklist.
- Keep real and synthetic data clearly separated in report exports and presentation materials.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `competition-demo-baseline`: extends the baseline with final submission artifacts and screenshot evidence.
- `intelligent-teaching-assistant-demo-package`: strengthens effect-report export, metric source, privacy, and asset-manifest requirements.
- `commercial-ui-governance-gates`: adds competition surface evidence requirements on top of unified secondary navigation governance.

## Impact

- Affects AppShell route conformance evidence, Playwright or browser screenshot scripts, effect-report API/page/export, competition documentation, asset manifest, privacy/model notes, and final validation commands.
- Depends on `freeze-competition-baseline`, `harden-assistant-evidence-loop`, `professionalize-document-grading`, `productize-learning-path-prep-pack`, and `enforce-secondary-navigation-visual-governance`.
- Does not add new AI capabilities or change core grading/path algorithms.
