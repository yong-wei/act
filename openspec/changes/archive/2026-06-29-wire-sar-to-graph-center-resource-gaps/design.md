## Design

Graph Center should request SAR associations for the selected node, objective, LearningGoal, and coverage refs. The payload can include event counts, top safe events, trace summary, candidate resource refs, and limitations. The UI should expose these as associated evidence and resource gap suggestions.

## Candidate Binding Policy

Candidate resources found through SAR must remain suggestions. They cannot become official graph bindings, ResourceNode mappings, path-eligible nodes, or verified citations until the relevant governance workflow reviews them.

## Visibility

Students can see only public or student-visible associated evidence. Teachers and administrators can see governance diagnostics and suggested resource gaps within their authorized scope.
