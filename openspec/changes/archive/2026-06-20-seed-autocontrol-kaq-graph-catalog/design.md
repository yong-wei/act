## Overview

The seed catalog should be enough to demonstrate and test K/A/Q graph-center behavior, while staying honest about incomplete course coverage. Existing runtime knowledge-node ids are preferred for knowledge bindings; capability and quality graph ids are new teaching-target constructs.

## Seed Scope

The initial catalog should include:

- Knowledge objectives across feedback concepts, modeling, time-domain analysis, root locus, frequency response, controller correction, and modern/frontier control.
- Capability objectives across modeling, analysis, synthesis, validation, engineering constraints, transfer, reflection, and AI collaboration.
- Quality objectives across safety responsibility, evidence integrity, model boundary awareness, system tradeoff, AI-use responsibility, continuous improvement, and ship-ocean mission context.

## Binding Policy

Knowledge graph seed nodes should bind to existing runtime ids such as feedback, transfer function, performance indicators, root locus, Bode plot, phase margin, PID, serial correction, simulation validation, and ship-control transfer nodes when present.

Capability nodes must bind to one or more knowledge nodes. Quality nodes may bind to knowledge nodes when relevant, but their primary contract is scenario, behavior, rubric, and evidence.

## Coverage Policy

The seed catalog may mark areas as partial when the existing runtime graph lacks a precise node. Partial coverage should be visible to tests and future resource coverage overlays.
