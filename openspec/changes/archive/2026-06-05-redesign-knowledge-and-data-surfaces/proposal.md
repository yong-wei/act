## Why

Knowledge graph and data center surfaces are high-value product differentiators, but current mobile structure and panel ownership still read as dense desktop tools squeezed onto smaller screens. They need a knowledge-data-map archetype with full-canvas focus, governed filters, evidence/status semantics, and mobile drawers.

## What Changes

- Redesign `/knowledge` as a canvas-first knowledge-resource workspace with mobile directory and filter drawers.
- Align `/data-center` and governance snapshots with knowledge/data map visual language, source quality, privacy scope, and freshness indicators.
- Preserve ResourceNode launch ownership and data truth boundaries.
- Verify selected knowledge nodes can launch registered resources, course entries, simulations, or evidence targets and can return to learning path or evidence review.
- Treat the known 320px split-panel squeeze-down failure as a blocking anti-pattern for knowledge/data map acceptance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `resource-node-knowledge-workspace-ui`: adds canvas-first and mobile drawer requirements.
- `platform-data-center-ui`: adds knowledge/data map and snapshot semantics.

## Impact

- Affects `/knowledge`, knowledge graph panels/filters/legend, `/data-center`, data snapshots, governance/data-center visual QA, and ResourceNode launch UI.
