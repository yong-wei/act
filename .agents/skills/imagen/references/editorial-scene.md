# Editorial Scene Reference

Use for article images, metaphor scenes, story visuals, conceptual illustrations, and narrative graphics.

## Prompt Structure

1. Core idea in one sentence.
2. Scene metaphor: concrete subject, setting, and action.
3. Composition: focal point, supporting elements, and reading direction.
4. Style: editorial illustration, cinematic still, documentary, collage, or stylized render.
5. Constraints: what facts, identities, or claims cannot be invented.

## Quality Rules

- Make the metaphor legible through objects and relationships, not explanatory text.
- Avoid generic business people, abstract shapes, or decorative filler unless requested.
- Use one clear narrative moment.
- If people are included, avoid implying real people unless provided.
- Keep text absent or minimal.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a [language if text appears] editorial image about [idea].
Canvas: [ratio].
Scene: [concrete setting and action].
Focal point: [main subject].
Supporting details: [objects that clarify the metaphor].
Style: [visual treatment, lighting, palette].
Visible text: [none or exact short text].
Negative constraints: no real-person likeness, no extra claims, no watermarks, no text paragraphs.
```
