设系统开环传递函数为$G(s)H(s)=\dfrac{K}{s^\nu}\prod_{i=1}^{N}G_i(s)$，其中$G_i(s), i=1,\cdots,N$一阶典型环节或二阶典型环节。系统开环对数幅频渐近特性为$L_a(\omega)=20\lg\left|\dfrac{K}{s^\nu}\right|+\sum_{i=1}^{N}20\lg|G_i(j\omega)|$。设在交接频率$\omega_i$处斜率的变化为$20k_i\,\mathrm{dB/dec}$，且有$\omega_1\leq\omega_2\leq\cdots\leq\omega_N$，则______。

- A. 当$\omega\leq\omega_1$时，$L_a(\omega)=20\left[\lg|K|-\nu\lg\omega\right]$
- B. 当$\omega_l\leq\omega\leq\omega_{l+1}, l=1,\cdots,N-1$时，$L_a(\omega)=20\left[\lg|K|-\nu\lg\omega+\sum_{i=1}^{l+1}k_i\lg\frac{\omega}{\omega_i}\right]$
- C. 当$\omega_l\leq\omega\leq\omega_{l+1}, l=1,\cdots,N-1$时，$L_a(\omega)=20\left[\lg|K|-\nu\lg\omega+\sum_{i=1}^{l}k_i\lg\frac{\omega}{\omega_i}\right]$
- D. 当$\omega>\omega_N$时，$L_a(\omega)=20\left[\lg|K|-\nu\lg\omega+\sum_{i=1}^{N}k_i\lg\frac{\omega}{\omega_i}\right]$

正确答案：C、D
