## 详情

### 完整解释

Various levels of simulation fidelity (accuracy) are available to the control engineer: low-fidelity simulations in early design stages using interactive design software with good graphics, where computer speed is less important; and high-fidelity simulations as the design matures, where processing speed becomes more important, typically programmed in FORTRAN, C, C++, MATLAB, LabVIEW or similar languages.
