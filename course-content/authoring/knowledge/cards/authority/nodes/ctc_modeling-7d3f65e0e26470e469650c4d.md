## 详情

### 完整解释

A five-step procedure: (1) determine the transfer function H(s), (2) determine the Laplace transform of the input U(s), (3) compute Y(s)=H(s)U(s), (4) break up Y(s) by partial-fraction expansion, (5) find y(t) by inverse Laplace transform.
