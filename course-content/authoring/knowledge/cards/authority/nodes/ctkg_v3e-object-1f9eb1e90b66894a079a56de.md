# Dominant Second-Order Poles Method

## 详情

### 完整解释

A pole-placement design approach that selects closed-loop poles for a higher-order system as a desired pair of dominant second-order poles, and selects the rest of the poles to have real parts corresponding to sufficiently damped modes, so that the system will mimic a second-order response with reasonable control effort.
