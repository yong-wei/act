# Circle Criterion

**一句话定义**：Circle Criterion：A frequency-response-based stability method that considers the energy seen at the terminals of the system

**核心直觉**：在图谱邻接中可把握：前置 → Sector conditions · 后续 → Frequency-Respon、External Stability、Nonlinear System。

## 详情

### 完整解释

A frequency-response-based stability method that considers the energy seen at the terminals of the system; if energy is always flowing into the terminals, the system is assumed stable. The result is expressed in terms of external properties such as the Nyquist plot of the linear portion facing the nonlinear elements.
