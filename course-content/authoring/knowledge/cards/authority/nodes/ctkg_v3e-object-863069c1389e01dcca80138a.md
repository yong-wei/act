# Marginal stability

**一句话定义**：Marginal stability：If the jω-axis roots of the characteristic equation are simple, the system is neither stable nor unstable

**核心直觉**：在图谱邻接中可把握：后续 → 稳定性。

**关联**：后续 → 稳定性

---

## 详情

### 完整解释

If the jω-axis roots of the characteristic equation are simple, the system is neither stable nor unstable; it is instead called marginally stable, since it has an undamped sinusoidal mode.
