# Separation principle

**一句话定义**：Separation principle：The principle that the full-state feedback law and the observer can be designed independently

**核心直觉**：在图谱邻接中可把握：后续 → Full-state feedback design procedure。

## 详情

### 完整解释

The principle that the full-state feedback law and the observer can be designed independently; the combined closed-loop system is stable if both the state feedback and observer error dynamics are stable.
