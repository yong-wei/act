## 详情

### 完整解释

In spacecraft attitude control, disturbances are torques that are ignored in the dynamic model and treated as disturbance signals affecting the system, such as aerodynamic torque and torques from other planetary bodies, magnetic fields, etc. The controller aims to minimize their effects.
