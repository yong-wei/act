### 完整解释

The natural frequency is denoted by ω_n and appears in the characteristic equation Δ(s)=s^2+2ζω_n s+ω_n^2=0. It is also used in the expression for the roots s_1,s_2=-ζω_n±ω_n√(ζ^2-1).
