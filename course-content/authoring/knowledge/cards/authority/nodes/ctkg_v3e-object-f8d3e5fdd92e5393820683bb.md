# Modified Matched Pole-Zero (MMPZ) Method

**一句话定义**：Modified Matched Pole-Zero (MMPZ) Method：A modification of the matched pole-zero procedure where the numerator of D_d(z) is of lower order than the denominator…

## 详情

### 完整解释

A modification of the matched pole-zero procedure where the numerator of D_d(z) is of lower order than the denominator by 1, so that the computer output u(k) depends only on input from the previous time e(k-1).
