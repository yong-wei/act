# Unstable System

**一句话定义**：Unstable System：A system where the characteristic equation has at least one root in the right half of the s-plane or repeated jω roots

**关联**：（权威图邻接待补充）

---

## 详情

### 完整解释

A system where the characteristic equation has at least one root in the right half of the s-plane or repeated jω roots; the output will become unbounded for any input.
