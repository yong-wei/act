## 详情

### 完整解释

The process of analyzing a system: write time-domain differential equations from physical laws, determine inputs and outputs, compute the transfer function, and then analyze the transfer function to determine dynamic properties (e.g., via pole-zero locations, time response to standard inputs, or inverse Laplace transform).
