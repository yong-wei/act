# Poles and zeros of T(s)

**一句话定义**：Poles and zeros of T(s)：The poles of T(s) are the roots of the characteristic equation Δ(s)=0

**关联**：（权威图邻接待补充）

---

## 详情

### 完整解释

The poles of T(s) are the roots of the characteristic equation Δ(s)=0; they determine the transient response modes. The zeros of T(s) establish the relative weightings of the individual mode functions.
