## 详情

### 完整解释

A method for studying stability based on the linear approximation: if the linear approximation is strictly stable (all roots in LHP), the nonlinear system has a region of stability around the equilibrium; if the linear approximation has at least one root in RHP, the nonlinear system cannot have any region of stability.
