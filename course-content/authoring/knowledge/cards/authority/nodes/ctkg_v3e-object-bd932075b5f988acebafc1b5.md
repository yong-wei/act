## 详情

### 完整解释

A digitization technique that approaches the problem as one of numerical integration using trapezoidal integration. It substitutes s = (2/T)*((1-z^{-1})/(1+z^{-1})) for every occurrence of s in D_c(s) to yield D_d(z).
