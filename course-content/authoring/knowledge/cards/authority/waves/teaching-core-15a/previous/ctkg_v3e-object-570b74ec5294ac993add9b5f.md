## 详情

### 完整解释

A system where the characteristic equation has simple roots on the imaginary axis (jω-axis) with all other roots in the left half-plane. The steady-state output will be sustained oscillations for a bounded input, unless the input is a sinusoid whose frequency is equal to the magnitude of the jω-axis roots, in which case the output becomes unbounded.
